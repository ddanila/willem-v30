WILLEM PCB5.0E HARDWARE SETUP
============================

STOP: the exact PCB5.0E socket position, board-version jumper position, and
external-power procedure still require confirmation.  Do not use this draft
kit for a physical chip until those items are replaced with verified settings.

The utility always keeps VPP disabled during read, blank-check, and verify.
It prints a numbered DIP diagram before applying chip power.  Follow the
numbers and the ON mark printed on the physical 12-switch bank.

2764/27C64 DIP MASK 12BH
  ON : 1 2 4 6 9
  OFF: 3 5 7 8 10 11 12

AT28C64 DIP MASK 128H
  ON : 4 6 9
  OFF: 1 2 3 5 7 8 10 11 12

FIRST PHYSICAL TEST
-------------------

Use the known original Juku 2764 and perform a read only:

  WILLEM R2764 JUKUROM.BIN 378 /TRACE

Keep WILLEM.LOG, WTRACE.BIN, and JUKUROM.BIN.  Compare the image on the modern
host before any EEPROM-writing command is implemented or enabled.
