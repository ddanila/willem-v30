WILLEM PCB5.0E HARDWARE SETUP
============================

STOP: On this 2015 PCB5.0E, adjacent three-pin headers J9 and J10 together
form the compatibility selector between the LPT logic chips near the DB25
connector.  Each has one shunt.  In the natural board orientation DB25 is on
the left and power is on the right.  Physical ROM reads proved this board is
reversed relative to its manual: PCB3B requires BOTH shunts toward DB25.
Each ==== below is a shunt:

PCB3B REQUIRED:   DB25 <- J9  o====o  o -> POWER
                          J10 o====o  o

WRONG/256 ALIAS:  DB25 <- J9  o  o====o -> POWER
                          J10 o  o====o

Ignore the manual's PCB35A/PCB3B direction for this specimen.  With the shunts
right, A8-A12 failed and every 256-byte page repeated.  With both shunts left,
all address pages read correctly.  Always move both together with power off.

Do not confuse J9+J10 with the two closed jumpers by OPEN=3.6V near the PLCC
sockets.  Leave the OPEN=3.6V jumpers closed.  Move no jumper while the board,
computer, or any cable is powered.

Place either supported 28-pin chip against the ZIF end opposite the lever.
Do not center it.  Leave two complete contact rows empty at the lever end:

ZIF PAIR:    1/32  2/31  3/30 ... 16/17
CHIP PAIR:    -/-   -/-  1/28 ... 14/15

The empty rows are contact pairs 1/32 and 2/31, both beside the lever.  Point
the chip notch toward those empty rows and the lever/pivot.  Chip pins 14/15
occupy the last socket row at the opposite end.  Use normal 28-pin routing,
normal parked large-device jumpers, and 5 V VCC.  Do not select a special
2716, 2732, 2816, 28F, 29C, or large-chip mode.

The utility always keeps VPP disabled, including during AT28C64 writes.
Before applying chip power it prints the ZIF placement reminder and a numbered
DIP diagram.  Follow the numbers and the ON mark on the physical switch bank.

2764/27C64 DIP MASK 12BH
  ON : 1 2 4 6 9
  OFF: 3 5 7 8 10 11 12

AT28C64 DIP MASK 128H
  ON : 4 6 9
  OFF: 1 2 3 5 7 8 10 11 12

FIRST PHYSICAL TEST
-------------------

The board needs its own power; LPT does not supply it.  With everything off,
select either USB 5 V or the DC jack, never both.  If using the DC jack, follow
its marked polarity and 9/12 V rating and set VCC to 5 V.  The source selector
is a separate two-shunt block marked ADAPT and USB; do not use J9/J10 for this.
Its location on this exact board still needs visual confirmation.  Connect a
straight-through DB-25 cable before powering either unit.  Power down before
changing the chip, cable, or any jumper.

The 12 V barrel-adapter setup has already powered this board successfully.
Keep that working setup unchanged and leave USB power disconnected.  Record
the adapter polarity and current rating from its label before the first run.

Use the known original Juku 2764 and perform a read only:

  WILLEM R2764 JUKUROM.BIN 378 /TRACE

Keep WILLEM.LOG, WTRACE.BIN, and JUKUROM.BIN.  Read twice and compare both
images on the modern host.  Only a passing validator run may create WRITE.OK;
without that file the EEPROM-writing command refuses to access the hardware.
